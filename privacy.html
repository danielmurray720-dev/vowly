Privacy policy

Vowly, the wedding planning app. Last updated November 25, 2026.

Vowly helps couples plan a wedding with the people they choose. We built it to keep your plan on your phone and share it only with the people you invite. We don’t show ads, we don’t use analytics or tracking, and we never sell or rent your information.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter (your checklist, budget, guest list, vendors, seating, timeline and notes) is saved on your phone. If you never turn on sharing, it never leaves your phone, except for backups you set up yourself (below).

When you share a wedding

If you turn on sharing or join a wedding with an access code, your wedding’s information is stored on Vowly’s server so the people you invite can see it. The server is run on Supabase, in the United States. You don’t create a password or give an email address: the app signs in with an anonymous account tied to your phone, plus an optional recovery key that only you hold.

The online RSVP page

If the couple shares an RSVP link, guests can find their invitation by name and send their reply. What a guest types (attendance, party size, meal, dietary needs, song request, shuttle pickup, email and a note) goes straight to that couple’s guest list. A guest can only see their own invitation. The page sets no cookies.

Backups

You can back up your plan to your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. Those copies go directly from your phone to your account with that service and are covered by its privacy policy. Vowly can only reach the Vowly folder it creates there.

Permissions

Vowly asks only when a feature needs it: the camera (disposable camera and receipts), photos (receipts), location (only while you share a shuttle’s position), calendars (only if you turn on “Show in my calendar”), notifications (reminders and alerts) and Face ID (if you turn on app lock). You can change any of these in your phone’s settings.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Shared wedding information stays on the server until you delete it. In the app, open Tools, then Sharing, then “Delete my account & server data”. That removes your account, your messages, the photos and files you uploaded, your alert registrations and your recovery key. If you are the last owner of a shared wedding, the whole shared wedding is deleted. No longer have the app? Email us from the address below with your wedding’s name and your display name, and we’ll delete your data within 30 days. Erasing the app’s data on your phone is separate: open Tools, then Settings, then “Erase this phone’s plan”.

Short technical records used to stop abuse (failed code attempts, alert delivery logs, RSVP rate limits) are deleted automatically within 7 days.

Children

Vowly is meant for adults planning a wedding and is not directed to children under 13.

Security

Connections to the server are encrypted. Each wedding is protected by access rules on the server, so people only see what their access level allows. Access codes expire and can be revoked by the couple.
